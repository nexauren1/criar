# ForgeKit Free Product Handoff

## Product

**ForgeKit — Free Developer Website Template**

## What the customer receives

The downloadable project contains the complete website source:

- HTML pages
- Shared CSS
- Shared JavaScript
- Customization guide
- Free-use template license
- Optional GitHub Pages workflow

## What the customer can do

They can use ForgeKit for personal or commercial end products, modify the source, create multiple projects and use it for client work.

## What they cannot do

They cannot redistribute or resell the original ForgeKit template itself as a competing template download.

## Before publishing a real site

Replace the sample brand, testimonials, pricing, contact behavior, demo logic and links.

## Product limitations

ForgeKit is a static front-end template. The included Demo Lab and Contact flow are browser-side demonstrations. Production applications may need a backend, API, database, email service, authentication or payment provider.
