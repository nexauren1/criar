04 · FAQ

Answer the questions before they become friction.

Can I use ForgeKit without a framework?

Yes. The starter is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can open it directly in a browser and deploy it on static hosting.

Can I change the colors and branding?

Yes. The CSS variables at the top of the stylesheet control the main visual system, and all brand copy lives in the HTML.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The navigation, grids, cards and spacing adapt down to small phone screens.

Can I connect a real checkout?

Yes. Replace the demo destinations with your checkout, app or payment links. No payment provider is bundled.
